FOUNDER & LEAD CINEMATOGRAPHER Rohit Suryawanshi
THE STORYTELLER

Crafting Films That Outlive The Moment.

Based in the historic city of Kolhapur, Maharashtra, I started MH09RS Films with a singular obsession: to free wedding photography from stiff poses and predictable formulas, transforming it into an immersive cinematic art form.

Weddings are not photo shoots — they are real family milestones overflowing with quiet nervous glances, unbridled laughter, sacred traditions, and unrestrained joy. My focus is to be present without intruding, anticipating raw emotion before it unfolds.

When I'm not behind the camera at weddings, you will find me exploring the untamed beauty of the Western Ghats — Amba Ghat, Radhanagari, and remote monsoon cascades. This deep connection to natural light and atmospheric landscapes directly informs our editorial color palette.

OUR VALUES

The Three Pillars of Our Work

01

Unforced Authenticity

We never interrupt real moments to create staged poses. The magic of your film lives in how you actually felt, not in artificial performance.

02

Cinematic Craft

We film in 4K resolution with cinema lenses, delicate natural lighting, licensed orchestral scores, and bespoke color grading reminiscent of independent cinema.

03

Generational Heirloom

Your film is edited to stand the test of time. Decades from now, you and your family will watch it and feel the exact same rush of love.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Where are you located and do you travel?

We are based in Kolhapur, Maharashtra, and regularly shoot weddings across Sangli, Satara, Pune, Mumbai, Goa, and destination wedding venues throughout India and abroad.

How far in advance should we book?

Because we take a limited number of celebrations each season to maintain the highest quality, most couples reach out 4 to 9 months before their wedding date.

What is your delivery timeline?

We provide social media teasers within 72 hours of your wedding. Complete color-graded highlight films and edited photography galleries are typically delivered within 6 to 10 weeks.
